# Card Velocity Attack

## Indicators
Many small transactions on one card within minutes, often on online retail or digital goods. Typical of card-testing and cash-out after a card number leak. Device ID is usually new for the customer.

## Recommended action
Temporarily block the card, contact the customer on a verified number, and review the device and IP used. Check whether other cards share the same device.

# Sudden Amount Spike

## Indicators
A single transaction many times larger than the customer's usual spend, often on electronics or high-resale goods. Common in account takeover and stolen-card use.

## Recommended action
Hold the transaction for step-up authentication. Compare against the customer's median spend and recent merchant categories.

# Unusual Geography At Night

## Indicators
Transactions from a country the customer has never used, in the early hours of the local home time, with travel or luxury merchants. Two or more in quick succession suggests card-present cloning or credential theft.

## Recommended action
Ask the customer whether they are travelling. If not, block the card and reissue. Check for a matching login from the same region.

# Structuring Below Threshold

## Indicators
Several payments just under a reporting threshold of 10,000 spread over hours or days. Used to avoid automatic review. Amounts cluster between 9,000 and 9,999.

## Recommended action
Aggregate the payments across the window and escalate to the compliance team for a suspicious activity review. Do not tip off the customer.

# Investigation Report Standards

## Required content
Each case report must state the transaction IDs reviewed, the evidence for each red flag with numbers, the suspected typology, the recommended action and what could not be verified. Avoid stating fraud as certain; use risk language.

# False Positive Guidance

## Common benign causes
A genuine large purchase after a salary credit, a customer who is travelling, a bill payment batch or a subscription renewal. Always check for a prior history of similar behaviour before closing a case as fraud.
